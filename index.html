Biography

Ahmed Elbossily is a Research Assistant and Ph.D. candidate working on high-performance computational methods for simulating manufacturing processes. His research focuses on GPU-accelerated particle-based methods, numerical methods, and computational mechanics.

Ahmed Elbossily develops C++/CUDA simulation solvers for large-scale Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) simulations, with particular interest in friction-based manufacturing processes such as friction extrusion, friction surfacing, friction stir welding, and refill friction stir spot welding. His work combines GPU programming, constitutive modeling, thermo-mechanical coupling, and contact mechanics.

His broader interests include high-performance computing, GPU algorithms, parallel programming, and performance optimization.
